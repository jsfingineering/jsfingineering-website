About JS FINGINEERING

JS FINGINEERING stands at the intersection of finance and engineering, providing integrated professional services designed to support performance, risk management and informed decision-making.

Company profile

Multidisciplinary by design.

Established on 22 July 2017, JS FINGINEERING is positioned as a multidisciplinary consultancy serving financial, engineering, valuation, due diligence, management, business and technology requirements.

Our integrated approach is intended for assignments where technical feasibility, financial viability, asset value, project risk and business objectives need to be considered together.

Master Scope: Multidisciplinary Financial, Engineering, Valuation, Due Diligence, Management and Business Consultancy Services, including Financial Advisory, Compliance Advisory and Assessment, Engineering Design and Analysis, Independent Engineer Services, Techno-Economic Viability Studies and Project Evaluation, Survey, Inspection and Assessment Services, and Generative AI Services.
